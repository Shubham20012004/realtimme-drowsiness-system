# Evidence informing the evaluation

| Publication | Method and data | Reported finding | Implication and limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soukupová and Čech, *Real-Time Eye Blink Detection Using Facial Landmarks*, CVWW 2016 ([author catalogue](https://fel.cvut.cz/en/faculty/people/444-jan-cech/publications)) | Temporal SVM over landmark EAR on two blink benchmarks | Detected short blink patterns and compared with existing blink methods | Supports a temporal eye baseline. Blink classification has a different reference outcome from vigilance. No DOI confirmed. |
| Ghoddoosian, Galib and Athitsos, *A Realistic Dataset and Baseline Temporal Model for Early Drowsiness Detection*, CVPR Workshops 2019 ([paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPRW_2019/html/AMFG/Ghoddoosian_A_Realistic_Dataset_and_Baseline_Temporal_Model_for_Early_Drowsiness_CVPRW_2019_paper.html)) | Hierarchical multiscale recurrent network using blink sequences. Five participant folds, 60 people and three video classes | Authors report higher classification accuracy than their human judgment baseline | Preserves cross-participant evaluation. Self-reported video labels cannot identify exact event onset. |
| Abtahi, Omidyeganeh, Shirmohammadi and Hariri, *YawDD: A Yawning Detection Dataset*, ACM MMSys 2014 ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/2557642.2563678), [dataset](https://doi.org/10.21227/e1qm-hb90)) | In-car videos from two camera positions with yawning, talking and other mouth movement | Dataset protocol exposes viewpoint and behaviour variation | Use for mouth-event specificity. It does not independently establish physiological drowsiness. |

**Proposed contribution to test.** Measure false alerts, event sensitivity and unavailable time jointly when quality gating and causal calibration are varied on identical subject splits. A publication requires evidence that gains survive the coverage trade-off. These three references support the design choices but do not establish priority or exhaust the literature.
